# Contributing to PAJ-Eval

PAJ-Eval is currently an experimental research instrument. The most valuable contributions are not feature requests; they are attempts to break the construct.

## Especially useful feedback

Please open an issue if you can show that:

- a simple fixed heuristic performs close to the oracle across worlds,
- the benchmark rewards Bayesian-game literacy rather than research judgment,
- two superficially similar worlds can be solved by the same shortcut despite different latent causes,
- the proposed assistance treatments differ in information quantity, latency, effort, or model capability,
- the primary score is fragile to reasonable cost/reward perturbations, or
- an expert would regard a benchmark-optimal trajectory as bad research practice.

## Reproducibility reports

Include Python version, OS, commit SHA, command, and the smallest trace needed to reproduce the issue.

## Research collaborations

For expert walkthroughs, counterfactual environment design, or post-assistance human studies, open an issue describing the specific failure mode or experiment you want to examine.

The project deliberately treats null results and failed hypotheses as useful outcomes.
