WHY THIS EXISTS

Same task success can hide different future humans.

Probe Player makes judgment observable as behavior. PAJ-Eval then asks whether an AI interaction policy changes the person's later ability to notice, frame, investigate, revise, stop, and act after the assistant is gone and the next problem has not been specified.
